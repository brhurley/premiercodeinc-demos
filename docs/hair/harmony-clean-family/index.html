Why Choose Us

What Makes Harmony Different

We combine traditional GShim Taekwondo with a warm, family-centered approach that builds character as much as physical skill.

Certified GTMA Instructors

Our instructors are certified through the Global Traditional Martial Arts organization and trained directly in the GShim Taekwondo curriculum developed by Grand Master G.K. Lee.

Family-Focused Training

From our Little Warriors to our adult athletes, we create a supportive environment where entire families can train, grow, and bond together on and off the mat.

Character Development

Our curriculum builds confidence, discipline, respect, and perseverance. We develop leaders who carry the tenets of Taekwondo into school, work, and life.

Competition Ready

Students compete in GTMA-sanctioned tournaments with GShim-specific forms, sparring, and weapons divisions. Our team brings home medals at every level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started at Harmony Martial Arts Academy.

GShim Taekwondo is a comprehensive martial arts system developed by Grand Master G.K. Lee, combining five decades of traditional Taekwondo expertise with modern martial arts principles. Taught through the GTMA (Global Traditional Martial Arts) organization, GShim includes its own unique patterns (forms), self-defense applications, and weapons training (nunchuck, sword, bo staff, kama). It emphasizes both the physical and philosophical principles of traditional Taekwondo while providing a world-class structured curriculum from white belt through black belt and beyond.

Our instructors are experienced in working with first-time students and will ensure your child feels comfortable and confident from their very first class. We offer a free trial class so your family can experience our training environment before committing.
For your first visit, comfortable athletic clothing like a t-shirt and sweatpants or athletic pants is perfect. No shoes are needed -- we train barefoot on padded mats. Once you enroll, we will provide a traditional dobok (uniform) as part of your membership. Please bring a water bottle and arrive about 10 minutes early so we can get you settled and introduced to the instructor.
The journey to black belt in GShim Taekwondo typically takes 3 to 4 years of consistent training, though this varies by individual. The GTMA color belt system has structured requirements at each level, including forms, techniques, sparring proficiency, and character development milestones. We believe the journey is just as important as the destination -- every belt level brings new skills, challenges, and personal growth. Students test when they are ready, not on a fixed timetable.
