About Us

Building Confidence, Discipline & Fighting Spirit

Training Martial Artists of all ages and abilities is our goal and our passion. Hair's K3 Martial Arts has been training students from White Belt to Black Belt in Sachse for over a decade.

With 18 years of experience in martial arts, focusing on Gshim Taekwondo, we seek to instill a fighting spirit and sense of confidence that will carry through all aspects of your life. Whether you are 3 years old or well into adulthood, our programs are designed to meet you where you are and help you grow.

We offer a range of martial arts programs designed for every age, skill level, and ability. Find the right fit for you or your family.

Gshim Taekwondo

Our core discipline rooted in traditional Korean martial arts. Develop striking techniques, forms, and the mental discipline that defines a true martial artist.

Self Defense

Special Needs Training

Inclusive training for students with cognitive and physical needs. Our adaptive approach ensures every individual can experience the benefits of martial arts.

Kids Programs

Adult Programs

It is never too late to begin your martial arts journey. Our adult classes focus on fitness, self-defense, stress relief, and personal growth at every level.

Our Journey

History & Milestones

From humble beginnings to a thriving martial arts community, our journey has been defined by growth, dedication, and the pursuit of excellence.

2008

The Journey Begins

Mr. and Mrs. Hair begin their martial arts training, discovering a passion that would shape their lives and the lives of countless students in the years to come.

2008 - 2015

Training Under Master Joy Walker

For seven formative years, Mr. and Mrs. Hair trained under the guidance of Master Joy Walker, studying under Grandmaster Robert Allemier and building a deep foundation in traditional martial arts.

2015

Hair's K3 Martial Arts Founded

Hair's K3 Martial Arts opens its doors in Sachse, TX, beginning its mission of training martial artists of all ages and abilities at the Michael J Felix Community Center.

2015 - 2020

American Taekwondo Association

Connected with the American Taekwondo Association, the school grows its student body and reputation throughout the Sachse community, training students from White Belt through Black Belt.

2021

Joining GTMA & K3

A new chapter begins as Hair's K3 joins Global Traditional Martial Arts (GTMA), training under Grandmaster Vivison Kerr, the K3 Founder. This partnership elevates the school's curriculum and global reach.

2025

GTMA Global Celebration

Hair's K3 Martial Arts participates in the GTMA Global Celebration, standing alongside martial artists from around the world and reaffirming our commitment to excellence in the global martial arts community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. Here are the most common questions from new and prospective students.

We welcome students as young as 3 years old in our Little Dragons program. Classes are structured by age group so every child receives instruction appropriate for their developmental stage. Our youngest students focus on coordination, listening skills, and basic movements in a fun, supportive environment.

Absolutely. We are proud to offer inclusive training for students with both cognitive and physical needs. Our instructors adapt techniques and pacing to ensure every individual can experience the physical, mental, and emotional benefits of martial arts. We believe martial arts is for everyone.

Registration is handled through the City of Sachse Active Communities portal. Visit their website to browse available sessions, select the class that matches your age group, and complete your registration online. Feel free to contact us if you need help with the process.

For your first class, comfortable athletic clothing is perfect -- a t-shirt and sweatpants or athletic pants work well. No shoes are needed as we train barefoot. Once you are enrolled, we will help you get set up with the proper uniform (dobok).

Gshim Taekwondo is a traditional Korean martial art that emphasizes discipline, respect, and personal growth alongside physical techniques. "Gshim" translates to "fighting spirit" -- the indomitable mindset that carries you through challenges both on and off the mat. Our curriculum includes forms (poomsae), sparring, self-defense techniques, and mental conditioning.

It is never too late. Our adult and teen classes welcome beginners and experienced students alike. Many of our adult students started with no martial arts background and have progressed through the ranks. You will train alongside supportive peers, build fitness and flexibility, and develop self-defense skills at your own pace.
