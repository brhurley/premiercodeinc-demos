April 13 · 9 AM – 5 PM

Fundamentals of Software Engineering In the Age of AI

Led by Dan Vega & Nathaniel Schutta

Room: La Frontera I

Master the essential software engineering practices that remain critical in the AI era, from design principles to effective collaboration with AI-powered tools.

April 13 · 9 AM – 5 PM

Building a Production-Grade RAG Pipeline

Led by Wesley Reisz

Room: La Frontera III

Build a complete retrieval-augmented generation pipeline from scratch, covering document ingestion, vector stores, chunking strategies, and production deployment patterns.

AI-Driven API Design
