About Us

Built on Tradition.
Driven by Excellence.

Iron Phoenix Martial Arts is Austin's premier GShim Taekwondo academy, proudly affiliated with Global Traditional Martial Arts (GTMA). Founded on the belief that martial arts training transforms lives, we provide a welcoming, disciplined environment where students of all ages discover their inner strength.

Our curriculum is rooted in the GShim Taekwondo system developed by Grand Master G.K. Lee -- a world-class program built on five decades of traditional taekwondo combined with modern martial arts principles. Every class emphasizes not just physical technique, but the character development, respect, and mental resilience that define a true martial artist.

Whether you are a parent seeking confidence-building activities for your child or an adult looking to challenge yourself, Iron Phoenix offers a path for you.
