## Executive Summary

GShim Taekwondo is a martial arts system developed by Grand Master G.K. Lee (Gyung Kun Lee), taught through the GTMA (Global Traditional Martial Arts) organization headquartered in Little Rock, Arkansas. GTMA has become one of the fastest-growing martial arts organizations in the United States, with over 100 affiliated schools across 32+ states.

The research identified the GTMA affiliate network as the primary vehicle for GShim Taekwondo instruction, with schools ranging from single-location dojangs to multi-location academies. Website quality among affiliates varies significantly -- from modern, conversion-optimized designs to more basic informational sites. The overall trend in martial arts web design for 2025-2026 emphasizes dark mode aesthetics, video hero sections, mobile-first design, community-centric layouts, and AI search engine optimization.

## About GShim Taekwondo

### Founder: Grand Master G.K. Lee (Gyung Kun Lee)

- **Born:** 1957, Daegu City, South Korea
- **Martial Arts Start:** Began training in second grade
- **First Black Belt:** Tang Soo Do at age 12
- **Military Service:** Korean Army 1978-1981 (taught Taekwondo)
- **Credentials:** 9th degree black belt in Taekwondo, Mugayeahru Kendo master, Professor in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, Tai Chi and weapons master, pioneer of combat stick sparring in Taekwondo
- **Philosophy:** Advocate of studying multiple martial arts -- "if there is always more to learn, then why limit yourself to training in one style"

### What is GShim Taekwondo?

GShim Taekwondo is the primary curriculum style supported by GTMA. It was specifically developed by Grand Master G.K. Lee to provide a world-class curriculum built on five decades of traditional taekwondo combined with modern martial arts systems. The system encompasses:

- Physical and philosophical principles of traditional taekwondo
- A structured color belt system progressing toward Black Belt certification
- GShim-specific patterns (forms) used in closed-circuit GTMA tournaments
- Self-defense applications
- GShim Weapons Training (nunchuck, sword, bo staff, kama)

### GTMA (Global Traditional Martial Arts)

- **Headquarters:** 10600 Colonel Glenn Road #500, Little Rock, Arkansas 72204
- **Phone:** 1-855-937-4862
- **Email:** info@gtmaonline.com
- **Websites:** gtmaonline.com (main), gtmaaffiliates.com (school locator)
- **Mission:** "Love the art. Preserve the craft. Empower the future."

GTMA offers three core program tiers:
1. **Shadow Moogi** (Pre-School, ages 4-6): Martial arts fundamentals with life skills
2. **Dragon Squad** (Elementary, ages 7-12): Progressive physical training and life skills
3. **GTMA Athlete** (Teen/Adult, ages 13+): Full GShim Taekwondo curriculum

## Martial Arts Website Design Trends 2025-2026

### Major Design Trends

#### 1. Experience-Focused Marketing
Modern martial arts websites are moving beyond basic class listings to showcase the full lifestyle and community experience. The best sites sell the transformation, not just the class.

#### 2. Dark Mode Aesthetics
Dark backgrounds with bold typography and rich imagery have become the dominant aesthetic. Dark themes convey authority, focus, and premium quality. Key color combinations include:
- Black/charcoal with red accents (most common in martial arts)
- Dark backgrounds with white/gold text
- Rich gradients over dark base colors

#### 3. Video Hero Sections
Full-screen hero sections with immersive video showing real workouts, real energy, and real students create instant connection and emotional impact.

#### 4. Mobile-First Design
With the majority of prospects searching on smartphones, mobile-first responsive design is no longer optional. Sites must provide seamless experiences across all devices.

#### 5. Community-Centric Layouts
Testimonials and reviews dominate modern layouts. The emphasis is on showcasing deep student loyalty, instructor trust, and emotional transformation stories.

#### 6. AI Search Engine Optimization
A significant new trend: websites must now be optimized for AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) -- not just traditional search engines. Your website is your school's first impression in AI search results.

#### 7. Conversion-Focused CTAs
Strategically placed calls-to-action above the fold, with urgency-based messaging ("Limited Time," "Schedule Your FREE Trial"), drive lead generation.

#### 8. Age-Based Program Segmentation
Clear program pages segmented by age group (Tiny Tigers, Kids, Teens, Adults, Competitors, Families) enable visitor self-selection and improve conversion rates.

#### 9. Hyper-Local Content
Multi-location studios create dedicated landing pages for each community, improving local SEO and personal connection.

#### 10. Emotional Storytelling
The best sites feature emotion-rich testimonials where students speak about confidence, transformation, personal growth, and community belonging.

## Must-Have Features for a Professional Martial Arts Website

### Foundation Features (Essential)

1. **Compelling Home Page**
   - Strong headline with unique value proposition
   - Professional action photos/videos of students and instructors
   - Clear CTAs above the fold ("Schedule Free Trial," "View Schedule")
   - Mobile responsive, fast loading

2. **Detailed Programs/Classes Pages**
   - Segmented by age group (Pre-School, Kids, Teens, Adults)
   - Comprehensive descriptions with benefits
   - Instructor profiles with qualifications
   - Visual demonstrations (photos/videos)

3. **Training Schedule**
   - Easy-to-read table or calendar format
   - Class times, days, program types clearly listed
   - Instructor assignments per class
   - Filter/search functionality
   - Downloadable PDF option

4. **Online Registration & Booking**
   - Free trial/introductory class booking
   - Secure payment integration
   - Digital waiver forms
   - Automated confirmation emails
   - Mobile-optimized forms

5. **About Us / Instructor Bios**
   - School history and founding story
   - Detailed instructor biographies with credentials
   - Mission statement and philosophy
   - Awards and recognition
   - Community involvement

6. **Contact & Location**
   - Phone number prominently displayed
   - Embedded Google Map
   - Contact form
   - Operating hours
   - Social media links

7. **Testimonials & Social Proof**
   - Student/parent testimonials (video preferred)
   - Google review integration
   - Before/after transformation stories
   - Star ratings display

### Growth Features (Competitive Advantage)

8. **Blog / Content Marketing**
   - Martial arts tips, techniques, news
   - SEO-optimized posts for local search
   - Regular posting schedule
   - Social sharing buttons

9. **Member Portal**
   - Training video library
   - Belt rank requirements
   - Event calendar
   - Payment history
   - Online fee payment

10. **Online Store**
    - Branded merchandise
    - Training equipment
    - Secure payment processing

11. **Appointment Scheduling**
    - Real-time class availability
    - Automated reminders
    - Integration with GloFox, MindBody, or similar platforms

12. **Multilingual Support**
    - Especially important in diverse communities

### Technical Requirements

- **Mobile-first responsive design** (non-negotiable)
- **Fast page load speeds** (under 3 seconds)
- **SSL certificate** (HTTPS)
- **Local SEO optimization** (Google My Business, local citations)
- **Analytics integration** (Google Analytics 4)
- **Schema markup** for local business
- **Accessibility compliance** (WCAG 2.1)
- **AI search optimization** (structured data, clear content hierarchy)
