Our Story

40+ Years of Tail-Wagging Grooming

Since 1984, The Dog House All Breed Grooming has been Garland's trusted grooming destination. What started as a small neighborhood shop has grown into one of the most recognized grooming businesses in the DFW metroplex — and we still treat every dog like family.

Walk into our shop and you'll notice the difference right away: a warm fireplace in the entry, the scent of natural shampoos, and a team that genuinely loves what they do. We've been named one of the 3 Best Pet Groomers in Garland for over 9 consecutive years — and we earn that reputation every single day.

Rick Holloway
Owner & Master Groomer
