40 Years of Wagging Tails

When Rick Holloway opened The Dog House in 1984, Garland was a different place. But some things haven't changed: neighbors still want a groomer they can trust, a place where their dog is treated like family, not a number on a schedule.

Four decades later, we still operate on that same promise. We specialize in every breed and every temperament -- from the anxious rescue who needs extra patience to the senior dog who deserves gentle, expert care. Our team of nine experienced groomers knows that every dog is different, and every dog deserves to go home looking great and feeling loved.

Named one of the "3 Best Pet Groomers in Garland" for nine consecutive years, with a 93 out of 100 Neighborhood Favorite score on Nextdoor. We're proud of the recognition, but what matters most is seeing tails wag at pickup.

Every Dog Goes Home Happy

It started years ago as a simple gesture -- a treat and a fresh bandana for every pup at pickup. Somewhere along the way, it became our signature. Parents tell us their dogs perk up at the sound of the treat bag rustling. Kids run to the door to see what color bandana their best friend is wearing this time. It's a small thing, but the small things are what make a grooming shop feel like home.
