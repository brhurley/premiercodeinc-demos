Our Story

A Neighborhood Tradition

When Rick Holloway opened The Dog House in 1984, he had a simple belief: every pet deserves to be treated with patience, gentleness, and genuine care. Over four decades later, that philosophy hasn't changed.

We've groomed puppies who grew into seniors, welcomed the rescue dogs who needed a little extra patience, and earned the trust of families who've been bringing their pets to us for generations. Garland is our home, and its pets are our family.

Every dog goes home with a fresh bandana and a treat -- because leaving The Dog House should feel like leaving a friend's home.

Rick Holloway

Owner & Head Groomer, Since 1984
