Our Story

Built on Trust, One Pet at a Time

Founded by Rick Holloway in 1984, The Dog House has been Garland's go-to grooming destination for over four decades. What started as a passion for working with animals has grown into a team of 9+ dedicated groomers who treat every pet like their own.

We specialize in working with anxious dogs, senior pets, and rescues who need a little extra patience and care. Our experienced groomers know how to make every visit comfortable, whether it's a nervous first-timer or a loyal regular.
