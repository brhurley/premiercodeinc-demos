Our Story

Four Decades of
Gentle Hands

When Rick Holloway opened The Dog House in 1984, he had a simple belief: every dog deserves to be treated with the same patience and care you'd give your own. That philosophy hasn't changed in over 40 years.

What started as a small grooming shop on Belt Line Road has grown into one of Garland's most trusted names in pet care. Generations of families have brought their dogs and cats through our doors, knowing they'll be handled with expertise and genuine affection.

We specialize in making every pet feel safe, from the bounciest puppy to the most anxious rescue. Our experienced team takes the time to understand each animal's needs, because grooming should never be stressful. It should feel like a spa day.

R
Rick Holloway
Owner & Head Groomer, Since 1984
