Our Story

Four Decades of Love,
One Dog at a Time

Founded in 1984, The Dog House All Breed Grooming has been a cornerstone of the Garland community for over four decades. What began as a small neighborhood grooming shop has grown into one of the most respected pet grooming salons in the Dallas-Fort Worth area.

Owner Rick Holloway and his team of experienced groomers understand that every dog has a unique personality and set of needs. Whether your pet is a nervous rescue finding their confidence, a senior dog deserving of gentle hands, or an energetic puppy getting their first groom, we treat every animal with the patience and care they deserve.

Every pup goes home looking their best and feeling loved -- complete with a fresh bandana and a treat, because at The Dog House, it's always about the little things that show we care.

Rick Holloway

Owner & Founder, Since 1984

Our Services

Expert Grooming for Every Breed

From full grooms to specialty treatments, we offer comprehensive care for dogs and cats of all breeds, sizes, and temperaments.

✂

Full Breed Grooms

Complete haircut and styling tailored to your dog's breed standard or your preferred look. All breeds, all sizes.

🚿

Bath & Brush-Out

Thorough bathing with premium shampoos, complete blow-dry, and professional brush-out for a healthy, shiny coat.

✨

Nail & Paw Care

Gentle nail clipping and grinding to keep paws healthy and comfortable. Perfect for regular maintenance visits.

💖

Ear & Teeth Cleaning

Ear cleaning and teeth brushing to support your pet's overall health and hygiene between vet visits.

🌿

Specialty Treatments

Oatmeal baths, conditioner treatments, hypoallergenic shampoos, and natural product options for sensitive skin.

🐶

De-Shedding & More

Furminator de-shedding treatments, flea treatments, and skunk odor removal. We handle the tough jobs with care.
